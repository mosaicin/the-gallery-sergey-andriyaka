# Museum research — Sergey Andriyaka

## Confirmed facts

Sergey Nikolaevich Andriyaka (1958–2024) was a Russian watercolor painter, teacher, People's Artist of the Russian Federation, academician of the Russian Academy of Arts, founder and artistic director of the School of Watercolor, and rector of the Academy of Watercolor and Fine Arts. He was born in Moscow on 14 July 1958 and studied painting at the Moscow State Academic Art Institute named after V. I. Surikov.

His best-known practice is multilayer watercolor painting. Public institutional materials describe his subjects as Russian architecture, historic cities, landscapes, interiors, still lifes, and flowers. The official academy biography explains that he developed layered glazing methods, often working through many transparent layers on dry or wet paper.

The Museum and Exhibition Complex of the Academy of Watercolor and Fine Arts is described by the official museum site as a state museum complex occupying four floors of the Academy, with exhibition halls totaling about 3,000 square meters. The museum site describes a permanent Andriyaka exhibition of more than 300 works and a watercolor museum collection of more than 250 works.
