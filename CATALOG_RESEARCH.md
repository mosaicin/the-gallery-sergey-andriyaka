# Catalog research: Sergey Andriyaka

Official source: https://mvk.academy-andriaka.ru/exhibitions/sergey-andriyaka/

The official permanent-exhibition page states that the updated exhibition, open since 4 April 2025, presents more than 300 works by Sergey Andriyaka from different years and stages of his career, all made in the classical multilayer watercolor technique.

The page publicly lists these 33 works, which are used as catalog metadata in the site. The site does not copy their protected images.
